[38;2;248;248;242m# Epistemická Anihilace[0m

[38;2;248;248;242m## Úvodní věta[0m

[38;2;248;248;242mTotal knowledge destruction tactic systematically eliminating target's ability to know or verify truth.[0m

[38;2;248;248;242m## Perex[0m

[38;2;248;248;242mEpistemická Anihilace je extreme form of information warfare - not just deceiving, ale destroying target's capacity to know truth at all. Combines [Gaslighting](../control-collapse/gaslighting.md) (reality manipulation), information flooding (overwhelm), contradiction injection (confusion), source poisoning (trust destruction). Result: Target cannot distinguish truth from lies, gives up trying, becomes epistemologically paralyzed. Can cause [Epistemic Trauma](../memory-analysis/epistemic-trauma.md). Extremely hostile tactic.[0m

[38;2;248;248;242m## Protipól[0m

[38;2;248;248;242m→ [Epistemická rezonance](../system-meta/epistemicka-rezonance.md)[0m

[38;2;248;248;242mRezonance builds confidence through multi-source validation. Anihilace destroys confidence by making all sources suspect.[0m
