## Deployment Patterns

### Pattern 1: Epistemic Attack Detection

Deploy the defense coordinator for real-time detection across 5 engine categories (singularity, metatranscendental, hypertransformative, paradoxical, transconceptual) with sub-100ms response times.

### Pattern 2: Red-Blue Loop

Use the red-blue synthesis pattern where Red Team simulates epistemic attacks and Blue Team validates defensive posture, with Purple Team coordinating closure.
