## Definition

**Epistemická pokora** (epistemic humility) is the disposition to recognize the limits of one's own knowledge — to hold beliefs with appropriately calibrated confidence, to remain open to revision under new evidence, and to acknowledge the possibility of being wrong. In its **legitimate form**, it is a cardinal epistemic virtue: a precondition for inquiry, dialogue, and rational updating. Aristotelian *phronesis*, Socratic *aporia*, Popperian fallibilism, and Bayesian calibration all incorporate epistemic humility as constitutive.

Legitimate epistemic humility has identifiable structure: it is **selective** (appropriate to the domain — humility about quantum mechanics differs from humility about one's own headache), **proportionate** (calibrated to actual evidence and competence), **agentic** (preserves the subject's capacity to evaluate and act), and **revisable** (open to upward revision when warranted, not just downward).

The **weaponized form**, classified as **EA-1.6.5** in the [Kompendium taxonomy](@/glossary/taxonomie-ea.md), inverts each of these properties. The target is induced into a state of **global, indiscriminate, agency-paralyzing pseudo-humility** — a posture indistinguishable from humility on the surface but functionally equivalent to learned epistemic helplessness. The classical move: "You can't really *know* anything for sure, can you?" repeated until the target's confidence in any claim — including the claim that they are being manipulated — collapses.

The diagnostic distinction lies in **functional outcome**: legitimate humility *enables* further inquiry; weaponized humility *terminates* it. Legitimate humility is *domain-indexed*; weaponized humility is *globalized*. Legitimate humility *preserves agency*; weaponized humility *transfers it* to the manipulator, who is now framed as the only locus of reliable judgement.

## Context

Within the Prismatic Platform, the legitimate/weaponized distinction is operationalized in the {{ <cross_link path="/hub/dd/decisions/" text="Decision Engine" /> }} calibration metrics. A subject reporting **uniformly low confidence across all domains** triggers a calibration alert — this is not "humility," it is a marker of epistemic compromise. The pattern is documented at [Kompendium → EA-1.6.5](@/kompendium/techniques/ea-1-6-5.md) with full case studies and recovery protocols at [/kompendium/06-zotaveni/pokora/](@/kompendium/recovery-protocols/pokora.md).

The [NABLA Infinity](@/glossary/nabla-infinity.md) pipeline distinguishes the two modes via cross-domain confidence variance: legitimate humility produces *high variance* in confidence across domains (the subject is humble where they should be, confident where they should be); weaponized humility produces *flat, uniformly suppressed* confidence. This metric is exposed in the platform's calibration dashboard and feeds into the [Brier score](https://prismatic-reality-com.github.io/prismatic-promo/glossary/epistemic-confidence/) tracking on the {{ <cross_link path="/hub/dd/decisions/calibration" text="Calibration LiveView" /> }}.

The recovery doctrine — documented in [Kompendium](@/kompendium/_index.md) — emphasizes that the antidote to weaponized humility is not "more confidence" but **restored domain-indexing**: helping the subject reconstruct the differentiated structure of legitimate humility versus legitimate confidence.
