## Definition

**Instrumental reason** (German: *instrumentelle Vernunft*) is the central diagnostic concept of Theodor Adorno and Max Horkheimer's *Dialectic of Enlightenment* (1944) and Horkheimer's *Eclipse of Reason* (1947). It names the historical degradation of reason from a faculty oriented toward **substantive ends** — truth, justice, the good life — into a faculty calculating only **efficient means** to externally given ends, with the ends themselves removed from rational scrutiny.

Adorno and Horkheimer's argument is that the Enlightenment's emancipatory project — the critique of myth and authority through reason — undergoes an internal **dialectical reversal**: in disenchanting the world, reason itself becomes a tool of domination. Once any end can be served by reason but no end can be evaluated by reason, reason is reduced to a calculating apparatus available to any project, including the most barbaric. The administrative rationality of fascism, the technical rationality of mass production, and the calculative rationality of the culture industry are not aberrations of reason — they are its terminal form once severed from substantive normative commitments.

Three features mark instrumental reason: (1) **means-ends asymmetry** — only means are rationally evaluated; (2) **identity-thinking** — qualitative differences are reduced to quantitative equivalence; (3) **subsumption** — particulars are subordinated to abstract categories without remainder.

## Context

The Kompendium uses Adorno-Horkheimer's diagnosis to explain a structural feature of contemporary epistemic annihilation: many EA techniques operate not by introducing irrationality but by exploiting **purely instrumental rationality** — coherent, calculating, technically efficient — uncoupled from any normative criterion that would identify the operation as harm.

Specifically:

- **EA-1.3.x immunization techniques** are often perfectly rational under instrumental criteria: each move efficiently serves the end of belief preservation. The pathology is not in the calculation but in the **fact that the end is exempt from evaluation**.
- **Mass-scale EA via algorithmic systems** (recommendation engines, ad targeting, predictive policing) is the instrumental-reason critique made operational: the systems are technically optimal for their stated ends, while the ends themselves systematically degrade the epistemic commons.
- The kompendium at [/kompendium/07-teoreticke-reflexe/](@/kompendium/07-teoreticke-reflexe/_index.md) pairs the Frankfurt-School diagnosis with [Habermasian communicative rationality](@/glossary/communicative-rationality.md) as the normative counter-move: communicative rationality is the contemporary heir to the Frankfurt project's attempt to recover a non-instrumental form of reason.
- The platform's [Decision Engine](/hub/dd/decisions/) explicitly tracks **which ends are being optimized** alongside **how well they are being optimized** — a Habermas-Adorno-aware design move that refuses to treat the optimization criterion itself as beyond audit.
- The [Color Teams](@/glossary/color-teams.md) framework includes Red Team scenarios in which the platform's own optimization machinery becomes adversarial to user epistemic interests — instrumental-reason auto-corruption tested as a threat model.
