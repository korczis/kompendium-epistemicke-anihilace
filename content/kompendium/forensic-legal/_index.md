# Forenzni a pravni rozmer EA

Sekce 50 kapitol propojujicich klinickou realitu epistemic abuse (EA)
s evidentiary standardy soudu. Strukturovano do peti tematickych bucketu
po deseti kapitolach.

## Bucket 1: Forenzni assessment (1-10)

Standardizovane nastroje a metodiky pro forenzni hodnoceni obeti EA —
od strukturovaneho rozhovoru pres credibility assessment a malingering
detection po komplexni dokumentaci.

## Bucket 2: Pravni ramce (11-20)

Doktrinalni zaklad pravniho prevedeni EA: undue influence, coercive
control statuty, defamation, conspiracy, fraud a profesionalni pochybeni.

## Bucket 3: Soudni svedectvi (21-30)

Praxe znalce a obeti u soudu: Daubert/Frye, witness protection,
cross-examination defense, jury instructions, judicial education.

## Bucket 4: Standardy dokumentace (31-40)

Forensne-akceptovatelna dokumentace: chain of custody, electronic
evidence, recording protocols, pattern evidence, mandatory reporting.

## Bucket 5: Specializovane pravo (41-50)

Kontextualni pravni oblasti: family court, immigration/asylum, employment,
disability, elder, child, religious exemption, trafficking, international.
