# EA-8.3.8 Trauma Recovery Literature

## Charakteristika zdroje

Shapiro (2018) EMDR Therapy. Levine (2010) In an Unspoken Voice (Somatic Experiencing). Schwartz (2021) No Bad Parts (IFS). Ogden, Minton & Pain (2006) Trauma and the Body (Sensorimotor). Cloitre, Cohen & Koenen (2006) STAIR/Narrative.

## Klíčové komponenty

Pět evidence-based trauma therapy modalities: EMDR (Shapiro), Somatic Experiencing (Levine), IFS (Schwartz), Sensorimotor (Ogden), STAIR/Narrative (Cloitre). Diverse phase-based approaches.

## Aplikace v EA kontextu

Operacionalizační literatura pro **EA-6.4.x specializované protokoly** a **EA-6.5.x krizové intervence**. Modality-matching: somatic → EA-5.x recovery; IFS → EA-3.x recovery.

## Validační status

EMDR: Shapiro 30+ RCTs. SE: Levine 200+ practitioners worldwide. IFS: Schwartz 50+ outcomes studies. STAIR: Cloitre 10+ RCTs (CPTSD). Evidence base growing.

## Limitace

Modality wars — ne všechny equally validovány (IFS méně RCTs než EMDR). Implementation requires specific certifikace. Cost-prohibitive in low-resource settings.

## Vztah k jiným zdrojům

Implementation vrstva pro **EA-8.2.10 Herman Recovery Stages** a **EA-8.2.9 van der Kolk Trauma**.
