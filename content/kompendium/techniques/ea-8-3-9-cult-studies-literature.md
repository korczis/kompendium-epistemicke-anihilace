## Charakteristika zdroje

Cultic Studies Review (ICSA peer-reviewed journal). Goldberg, Goldberg & Henry (2017) Cult Recovery: A Clinician's Guide. ICSA (International Cultic Studies Association) conference proceedings 1979-2024. Hassan (2020) Combating Cult Mind Control 30th Anniversary Edition. Lalich (2004) Bounded Choice.

## Klíčové komponenty

Pět key resources v cult studies oblasti: peer-reviewed journal (CSR), clinical guide (ICSA), conference proceedings (40+ years), updated foundational text (Hassan), academic monograph (Lalich).

## Aplikace v EA kontextu

Pole-specific resources pro EA framework. ICSA — největší professional cult studies organization. CSR — primární peer-reviewed journal. Annual conference — current state pole.

## Validační status

ICSA — 1000+ professional members. CSR — 30+ years peer-reviewed. Cumulative output: 5000+ articles, 200+ books napříč ICSA-affiliated authors.

## Limitace

Pole pole methodologically diverse (clinical, sociological, journalistic). Less standardized than mainstream psychology. Limited funding compared to mainstream trauma research.

## Vztah k jiným zdrojům

Pole-specific kontext pro **EA-8.3.1 Foundational Readings**. Komplementární s **EA-8.3.5 Historical Cases** a **EA-8.3.6 Contemporary Cases**.
